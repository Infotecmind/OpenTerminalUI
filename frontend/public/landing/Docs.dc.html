Getting started

FinInfo Terminal is hosted, so there is nothing to install. Open the site, sign in, and the terminal opens on Home.

—Sign in at fininfoterminal.tecsolutiongroup.com.
—Home is mission control: watchlist, news and your saved views on one screen.
—The left rail moves between areas; the top bar carries the GO bar and the command palette.
—Layouts persist as saved views, so the desk looks the same next session.

Finding a symbol

The GO bar is the fastest route to any instrument. It takes a ticker, a company name, or a command.

—Press Ctrl/Cmd+G from anywhere to focus it.
—Type a ticker to jump straight to that security page.
—Coverage spans equities, forex, commodities, bonds, mutual funds and derivatives.
—Right-click any symbol in the terminal for its context actions.

Command palette

Every action is reachable by name, so nothing has to be hunted for in a menu.

—Ctrl/Cmd+K opens the palette; start typing to filter.
—Ctrl/Cmd+/ lists every shortcut currently in scope.
—Ctrl/Cmd+9 opens the launchpad for moving between areas.

Charts and the workstation

The chart workstation holds several panels at once, each with its own symbol, timeframe and studies.

—Ctrl/Cmd+Shift+N adds a panel; Ctrl/Cmd+L changes the layout.
—Tab and 1-9 move focus between panels; F makes one fullscreen.
—Alt+1-7 switches timeframe from one minute through one month.
—On the focused pane: I indicators, D drawings, V volume profile, R replay.
—Replay steps the chart through history bar by bar, so a setup can be judged as it formed.

Research and screening

Screen the market, then take the shortlist straight into a chart or a portfolio.

—Screener builds filters on fundamentals, technicals and custom formulas.
—Heatmap, hotlists and relative strength show where the session is actually moving.
—Compare and correlation put several instruments side by side.
—Factors and Alpha Zoo test which signals carried return over a period.
—News carries sentiment scoring; Ctrl/Cmd+N opens it from anywhere.

Portfolio, paper trading and risk

Positions, simulated execution, and the record of why a trade was taken all live in one place.

—Portfolio tracks holdings and performance; Portfolio Lab backtests and blends strategies.
—Paper trading runs orders against live prices on virtual capital.
—Position sizer works the size back from the risk you are willing to take.
—Journal keeps the reasoning beside the fill, which is what makes a review worth doing.
—Risk and OMS cover exposure, limits and order state.

Watchlists and alerts

Let the terminal watch the levels you care about instead of watching them yourself.

—Ctrl/Cmd+W opens the watchlist outside the chart workstation.
—Alerts fire on price and on multi-condition rules.
—A opens the alert centre for the symbol in the focused chart pane.
